Who I am

I'm Carlos Mercado, founder of ERC Academy in Nicaragua and creator of the Auditory Activation & Neuroplastic Conditioning method.

I don't run a classroom. I train how people hear, remember, and respond, so English stops being something they study and becomes something they use.

What I offer

A long-term training program that rebuilds your relationship with English from the ground up, starting with sound. You won't get shortcuts or inflated promises. You get a structured, honest path with personal coaching, constant feedback, and a method designed so that real understanding and real speech come out of it.

The core principles

  1. Sound first, meaning later.

    Children hear a language long before they read it, so we start where the brain naturally starts.

  2. Language is live processing, not memorization.

    Knowing words on paper is different from understanding them at full speed, and we train the second.

  3. Neuroplasticity.

    With the right repetition, variety, and timing, the brain builds new pathways at any age.

  4. Beat the forgetting curve.

    Learning is timed so that what you practice stays with you.

  5. Chunks over rules.

    You absorb whole, natural phrases, and grammar settles in through use.

  6. Mind and body together.

    Voice, rhythm, and movement are part of how the learning sticks.

  7. Progress, not levels.

    We don't label you beginner or advanced. We measure effort, resilience, and how effortlessly you understand and respond.

  8. Honesty.

    No fast-track fluency claims. Results come from completing the work.

The value

  • You understand English when it's spoken, not only when it's written.
  • You speak with natural rhythm and clear pronunciation.
  • You recall language under pressure, when you need it.
  • You gain confidence, because you trust your ears and your mouth.
  • You get coaching and accountability, not a course you open once a month and forget.

A few honest questions

Why not start with grammar?

Because a child doesn't. Grammar arrives later, and it sticks because it's already familiar.

Will I understand everything at the start?

No, and you're not meant to. Understanding grows steadily, and it grows on a solid base.

Is this for everyone?

No.

Who this is for

Serious, committed learners with a clear goal and a personal reason for learning English, who are ready for discomfort and long-term growth and who take responsibility for their own progress. It isn't for advanced learners who want grammar fine-tuning. Every learner begins with a qualification interview, because the method works best for people who are ready for it.

This isn't babysitting vocabulary. It's rewiring habits, one sound at a time. If that's the kind of change you want, let's talk.
